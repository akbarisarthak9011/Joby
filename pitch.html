Who it serves · Why now

Early-career applicants adapting one CV to multiple roles. Job descriptions change; generic AI can produce polished claims with no proof.

First users: career communities, bootcamps, and university alumni groups. Start with user interviews and a small opt-in pilot.

Data is the product

Job snapshots + CV versions + agent traces make freshness and evidence inspectable.
